About
I am an M.S. Student at the School of Electronic and Computer Engineering, Peking University Shenzhen Graduate School, advised by Prof. Lingyang Song. 
Prior to this, I received my B.S. degree from the School of Electronics Engineering and Computer Science at Peking University in 2024.
My current research centers on AI-enabled edge computing, computer networks, and satellite communications, with a particular focus on resource orchestration for LEO serverless computing networks and zero-trust access control architectures.
